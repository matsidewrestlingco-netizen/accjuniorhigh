Tournament Weekend

Arrival & Check-In

Each team receives a packet upon arrival on Friday, January 8 containing:

  • Three (3) coach's bands
  • Your team's ACC weigh-in sheet
  • Any other pertinent event information

Bands are not distributed at the front entrance for late-arriving coaches. If you arrive without your team, coordinate with your coaching staff to get your band before entering the building.

This is not your OPC sheet (you manage those) — submit the ACC weigh-in sheet to the head table after your team weighs in.

About the Tournament

Allegheny County's Premier
Junior High Wrestling Event

Every January, the top junior high wrestling programs from across Allegheny County gather for two days of individual bracket competition. Wrestlers battle through a full weight-class tournament — round of 16, quarterfinals, semifinals, and finals — to earn a spot on the podium.

The tournament is run by three tournament directors — Drew D'Agostino, Adam Santoro, and Daniel Emmons — with coordination and support from the Shaler Area Wrestling Boosters, who host the event at Shaler Area High School.

This is a wrestler's tournament: no team scoring gimmicks, no shortcuts — just weight classes, brackets, and the chance to be named an Allegheny County champion.

Tournament Format

Full Bracket, Every Weight Class

Wrestlers compete in a single-elimination bracket by weight class, with a true fifth-place round so every wrestler who makes it that far knows exactly where they stand.

01

Weight-Class Brackets

Every entrant is seeded into a bracket for their weight class — from the round of 16 or larger down to the finals.

02

Championship & Consolation

Lose in the championship side and you're not done — the consolation bracket determines third and fifth place finishers.

03

County Champions Crowned

Finals matches on day two crown an Allegheny County champion in every weight class.
